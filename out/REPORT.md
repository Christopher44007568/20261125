# Hair Card Bake Report

Generated by the pure-Go-stdlib pipeline in `hairbake/`.

## Reduction

- Input strands: 680 (each 24 segments)
- Flyaway strands: 100 (14.7% of groom)
- Output hair cards: 10 (budget-priority: silhouette > highlight > interior detail)
- Mean strand-to-card-center distance: 2.164 units, max 8.606 units
- Silhouette IoU (reference vs cards, front view): 44.2%
- Highlight energy retention (specular pixel sum): 81.2%

## Flyaway audit

- Lost flyaways (tip outside ribbon width): 4 / 100
- Retained flyaways: 96
- Lost tips are ringed in red in `side_by_side.png`, `closeup.png` and `montage_views.png`.
- The card budget (10) forces wide silhouette ribbons; sparse outward arcs cannot be
  represented without dedicated cards, so their tips are the dominant loss.

## Texture seams

- Atlas: 1024x1024, 2x5 island grid with 10px empty gutters (see `atlas_annotated.png`).
- Yellow dashed lines mark island borders; gutters are intentionally unfilled so
  filtering at card edges exposes the seam in the card renders.
- Card-row views overlay the same dashed borders directly on the head (`montage_views.png`).
